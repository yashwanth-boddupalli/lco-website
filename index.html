FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the questions we hear most often.

How do I get a new cable TV or broadband connection? +
Call us at our helpline, fill the form on this website, or WhatsApp us. We'll verify your address and schedule an installation visit — usually within 24 hours.
What are the installation charges? +
Installation is free for most plans. A small one-time setup fee may apply for special configurations. Our team will inform you upfront — no surprises.
What broadband speeds are available? +
We offer plans from 25 Mbps all the way up to 200 Mbps. All plans come with unlimited data. Choose based on the number of devices and usage in your home.
How many TV channels will I get? +
Our basic plan starts with 150 channels. Premium plans offer 350+ channels including HD, sports, movies, and regional content. Check our Plans page for details.
How long does installation take? +
A standard installation takes 1–2 hours at your premises. You'll have a working connection the same day our technician visits.
How do I register a complaint? +
You can call our helpline, WhatsApp us, or use the Support page on this website to register a complaint. We typically resolve issues within 4 hours during working hours.
What payment options are accepted? +
We accept cash, UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm), bank transfer, and cheque. Our team can also assist you with payment when they visit your home.

Our Story

16 Years of Connecting
Our Community

SkyLink was founded in 2008 by a team of passionate local professionals who believed that quality cable TV and internet should be affordable and reliable for everyone — not just big cities.

Starting with just 200 cable TV customers in our neighbourhood, we have grown to serve over 8,500 homes and businesses across the area. Along the way, we've upgraded our network, added fibre-ready broadband, and — most importantly — kept the same personal, local touch.

When you call us, you speak to someone who lives in the same community. That's something no big national operator can offer.
